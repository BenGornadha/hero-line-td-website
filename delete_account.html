Delete Your Account — Hero Line TD

Last updated: April 8, 2026

How to delete your account

You can delete your account directly from the app:

  1. Open Hero Line TD
  2. Go to Options (gear icon)
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion

What data is deleted

When you delete your account, all of your data is permanently removed from our servers, including:

  • Your user profile (ID, email, display name)
  • Game progression (XP, level, talent points, heroes, campaign progress, gems)
  • Multiplayer history

Timeframe

Account deletion is immediate and irreversible. All data is removed as soon as you confirm.
