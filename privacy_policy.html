Privacy Policy — Hero Line TD

Last updated: March 13, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Hero Line TD, we may collect the following information:

  • Apple ID (via Sign in with Apple): A unique user identifier and optionally your email address and name.
  • Game Progression: XP, level, talent points, owned heroes, campaign progress, gems, and tutorial completion status.
  • Username: A display name you choose within the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your account and enable cloud save functionality.
  • Synchronize your game progress across devices.
  • Enable multiplayer matchmaking.
  • Display your username to other players during multiplayer matches.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on Supabase (AWS eu-west-2 london) servers. Data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encrypted connections.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. We do not use any advertising SDKs or analytics trackers.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from the app's Options menu. Upon deletion, all your data is permanently removed from our servers.

6. Children's Privacy

Hero Line TD does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
